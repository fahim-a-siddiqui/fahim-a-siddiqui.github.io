Object Number: 403
Description: O-403 is an entity that cannot be described by a sentient mind. It is speculated to be a humanoid creature that has extensive memory-erasing capabilities. Nothing can be written, recorded, remembered, analyzed, or thought about O-403 except for its preference to be called 'Mary' by the people it meets. Agents are requested to be vigilant and careful whenever meeting someone called Mary. Its preference of its name is the only thing that can be transmitted through this document, and therefore be stored in the human mind. 

Dear readers, it has been so long since I've last written here in this blog. I think I remember having a blog three hundred centuries ago, but back then it wasn't a blog on the internet per see; rather I wrote in a journal (by hand! such a thing's no longer relevant in this digital world of ours) and shared pages with friends; I assume you'd call that a blog, wouldn't you? Just not a blog on the internet. Maybe if I called it a subscription service it'd sound more appropriate, but saying so makes it sound like I'm company salesman trying to sell you something you really don't need. 

The station where I'm employed at is lively as ever. Well, not lively as you'd think. Our site is literally in the middle of nowhere, and by nowhere I mean a woodland forest where the trees get so tall it's hard to see the sky sometimes; it's all foliate up there. I get to see and hear woodland creatures everyday. Birdsong is a very pleasant morning alarm. There's also no need to commute to my job since we're expected to live at the office. Don't think we're cut off world, dear reader. The station has great internet coverage, and we get new faces occasionally thought not as often as I'd like. 

There isn't much to do at my job. Just ensure the objects stored at the facility don't escape containment. I sometimes dream of being an agent because they get to travel and the Department pays for all their expenses like the benefactor they truly are. I'd contain objects, and meet all sorts of people on the road, and have a partner who'd eat donuts all the time, and do all the typical activities that is expected of a long-running supernatural television. But I have to tell you, dear reader, that travelling like that around the world seems to get a little boring after you've done it for more than a thousand years. Travel long enough and the world seems pretty small. Anyways, I'm satisfied here. It'd be a much nicer place too, if only people remembered who I was. As usual, they only remember my name, but nothing else. My boss keeps on forgetting that I work here, so I try my best to avoid him. I love talking to people, but having to introduce myself every time we start a conversation can begin to feel very repetitive.

I've had this part of me since I was born. Hmm. It seems I can't remember when I was born. Well, I reckon it wasn't *that long* ago. Since I'm inflicted with this condition, I'm free to do whatever I want at the station. I could murder a child with my bare hands in front of my employees, and they'd certainly cause I scene, but give it enough time, and they'd forget. The only thing someone can ever remember about me is my name, so my existence is not set in stone. I don't have my certificate of birth since I was born before nationalism, and I don't have identification documents because information about me, other than my name, cannot be recorded. 

However, I can describe myself here. This blog has always felt very strange to me because every time I write here, I feel I'm passing through words and my words are being transmitted to a world where the strangeness and queerness of Objects don't exist. This blog, I dare say, could be an Object and I could be totally oblivious to it.

Martin, the young blonde man who only hears blonde-shaded clothing whenever I see him, passed by me today. He's quite handsome I say, but he strikes me as very strange. I don't know if its because of his eyes that skitter around everywhere, trying to take full breath of the room, or his poor fashion choices. I know he's trying to match his clothing to his hair, but that sort of thing is fine once a while. Not every time. He does it every day. I wonder how many blonde articles he has in his closet.

Martin glanced at me, and looked somewhere else. Seemed he wasn't interested in approaching the only stranger in the office. He then walked somewhere else, and I kept standing near the vending machine, my back leaning slightly on its cold metal rims. I watched some familiar faces pass by until I grew bored, and went to my office which was a room so deep and hidden in the building that even the cleaners had forgotten about it. The first time I remember finding the place, I was appalled at the amount of dust and filth that had accumulated over the years. I'd kidnapped one of the cleaners from their duty, and threatened to kill them if they didn't clean up the room in a day. Ah, I still remember just how much the man's hand shook whenever I'd point the pistol at him. I didn't know how a pistol worked, expect that you'd hold it at something you want to shoot at; the man never questioned that. I let him go after his work was done, and he totally forgot about me afterwards. Sometimes I thank God for my little gift. It makes it so easy to kidnap people.

My office was in the lower floors, below the basement. I didn't even know here was anything below the basement when I found it. It's a small room with walls of aluminum and decent air ventilation and cooling. There was a window to the right but it only showed dirt because the place was underground. Maybe the architect thought someone would enjoy looking at dirt whenever they'd go for a work break. Other than my desk and shelves stuffed of books and that three thousand dollar chair that I stole from my Boss when he was on leave for an important trip, there isn't any furniture in the room. It's quite bare actually. The emptiness is the most striking aspect of the room. Okay, after the window that shows dirt. It's the second, alright? 

I don't work on collaborations and because of my social isolation I don't have to deal with pesky meetings that are always the worst part of being employed anywhere. I spend my time reading files on Objects the Department has information, and occasionally going to the storage places to ensure none of the Objects have escaped. I don't have to check on all of the objects, only the ones with signs saying 'Anti-human memory Object.'. Those were the ones that are entrusted to me. Self-entrusted to me. It's me being nice to people as the other employees can't check on them. They're kept separate from the rest of the Objects which makes my work easier since I won't be questioned on my being there. It's never nice to have someone come up to me all rude-like and ask whether I'm an employee of here or not. I always say I am, but then they ask for identification which is a big bother for me. I've always had to use someone else's ID card to get access to restricted facilities. The way I deal with such people is simple. I make them forget about me at the instant, leaving behind only my name with them if I'd said them that (I cannot control that part of myself in someone else's memory), and avoid meeting them ever again. 

I sat down on the chair, and scanned the files I had strewn across my desk last night. One of them talked about O-507. I'll list the contents of the entry here. 

Object Number: 507

The term life form includes and is not limited to bacteria, animals, plants, and fungus. Viral bodies, as observed through a microscope, are not affect by 0-507 as a virus is not considered a living thing. It has the holes one normally prescribes to cheese bricks depicted so in cartoons, and weights a few grams. Affected bodies are, in every way, safe to eat as Department employees have testified. The taste of affected bodies has been described has 'cheesy' which is not very helpful, but it is better than nothing. O-507 is contained at an undisclosed station in the United States.

A brick of cheese! Now, I'd like to see that object for myself. Wonder how it tastes. If it's the last thing I can eat, then I'd gulp up it down without a sign of hesitation. I hope it'd taste good then.
